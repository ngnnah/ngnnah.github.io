> "We're drowning in information while starving for wisdom."
> — E.O. Wilson

Cal Newport's definition is the one I use: "focusing your online time on a small number of carefully selected activities that strongly support things you value, then happily missing everything else."

Four rules I actually follow:

- Newsletters over social feeds, books over browsing.
- No notifications before 10am or after 8pm.
- Three essential tools, not thirty productivity apps.
- Email twice a day.

The space between thoughts got bigger. Ideas connect when nothing interrupts them.

What I didn't expect: I enjoy technology more now. Good espresso versus bottomless diner coffee. The experience is richer for being bounded.

Design your information environment instead of inheriting it from an algorithm.
