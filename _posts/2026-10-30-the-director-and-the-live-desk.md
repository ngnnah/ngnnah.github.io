I run four or five Claude Code sessions at once. After ten turns in each, I couldn't say where any of them stood. The answers were buried in chat.

So each session now keeps a live desk: one markdown file with Status on top (now, open questions, next, decisions, done) and a log below. I read it in Obsidian and answer right at the line, with `>>`. Then I send `>>` in chat, and the session picks it up.

Two small hooks help. A bell nudges a session whose Status went quiet. A whistle says when a session is long enough to hand off to a fresh one.

I made two things to share it:

- [The >> Director](https://ngnnah.github.io/I-And-AI/projects/live-desk/), a picture-book deck I demoed at work. Look for the easter eggs.
- [Live desk setup](https://ngnnah.github.io/I-And-AI/projects/live-desk/setup/). One button copies a setup prompt; paste it into your agent and it installs the rules.

Built and used with Claude Code. Codex and Amp should follow it too; I haven't tried them.
